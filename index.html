Philippine public road contracts · open data · human review

Which road contracts deserve a closer look today?

BantAI reads every DPWH road contract each morning, checks it against patterns that past contracts show go with failure or complaints, and puts the ones worth checking in front of a person. The person decides.

A request to look, not an accusation. A flagged contract has a pattern worth checking. Most will have an ordinary explanation. This page shows totals only; no contract or company is named here.

Red — look first: an official record (blacklist, revoked licence, official action) or two strong patterns together.
Yellow — review: one strong pattern, or two weaker ones.

Grey — one random, unflagged contract a day, so we can tell whether the flags beat chance.

Why these flags

Each pattern was tested on thousands of past DPWH road contracts. The number is how many times higher the odds of the outcome were when the pattern was present (1× = no difference). Lines show the 95% range. Only patterns clearly above 1× are used to score; the rest are shown for context.

Odds ratios from case-control comparisons on DPWH records, 2018–2025; patterns were also re-checked on 2023–2025 alone. A pattern linked to an outcome is not proof that any one contract has a problem.

Where the data comes from

All public. Nothing is bought, and nothing about private individuals is collected.

Safeguards

People decide

  • Every case is a request for a human check.
  • The AI assistant reads sources and notes gaps; it never scores, decides or publishes.

Evidence before rules

  • A pattern scores only after it holds up on past contracts, including recent years.
  • Rule changes need a person's approval and are logged.

Fair to companies

  • Missing data is never counted against anyone.
  • Name matches (blacklist, officials, offshore records) are marked “verify”; namesakes are common.
  • No contract or company is named in public.
