Open construction tenders on PhilGEPS

Every construction tender open on the PhilGEPS notice board, read each morning: mostly provinces, cities, municipalities and barangays, which DPWH's data does not cover. Road works are marked. At tender stage they are not scored (no road lengths, no bidders yet), but each carries watch notes where the agency's past road awards were dominated by one firm or the tender is a re-bid. Once awarded, local road contracts are assessed with BantAI's rules and the flagged ones join the joint case list. A listed tender is not a finding of anything.
